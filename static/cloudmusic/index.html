THE APP

Meet Cloud Music.

Cloud Music Player - Listener brings your tracks together, helps you organize them your way, and keeps the music playing offline.

Download on the App Store ↗

Cloud Music helps you access music stored in Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive. You can also import your files through Wi-Fi transfer from a PC or Mac, with iTunes file sharing, or from Files.

☁

Your cloud library

Bring tracks from supported cloud services into one place and enjoy a more organized listening experience.

↓

Listen offline

Download songs to your device, view your offline list, and play without an internet connection.

♫

Playlists for every moment

Create, rename, and edit playlists to keep the right music close at hand.

⇄

Playback your way

Choose repeat or shuffle, keep music playing in the background, and use lock screen controls.

◷

Sleep timer

Set a timer when you want your music to stop automatically.

▣

Popular audio formats

Play common music files including MP3, M4A, and WAV.

Cloud Music is designed for music files you have access to. Availability of cloud services and playback features may depend on your device, file type, and connection.
