Your library.
More possibilities.

Apple CarPlay, offline listening, flexible imports, and personal playlists. Built around the music files you bring.

View on the App Store ↗
01

Apple CarPlay

Browse Songs and Playlists on a compatible CarPlay display and control playback while your iPhone stays connected.

02

Cloud drives

Connect Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive to browse and download the music files you choose.

03

Files & computer transfer

Import with Files, Wi-Fi transfer, Finder on Mac, Apple Devices on Windows, or iTunes on older computers.

04

Offline listening

Music imported onto your device is available without an internet connection. Cloud sign-in and new downloads require a connection.

05

Playlist management

Create, pin or unpin, rename, change cover artwork, and delete playlists. Deleting a playlist keeps its songs on your device.

06

Playback controls

Use shuffle, repeat, a playback queue, background playback, and lock screen controls.

07

Sound settings

Choose equalizer presets and sound environments to adjust your listening experience.

08

Sleep timer

Choose when playback should stop automatically, with quick presets and a custom timer.

09

Light & dark themes

Switch appearance modes and select from the corresponding theme colors.

10

Audio formats

Play MP3, M4A (AAC/ALAC), M4B, AAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, and more. Playback depends on the file’s encoding.
