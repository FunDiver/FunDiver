Privacy policy

At a glance

Personal records and document recognition stay on your device. Optional statistics and text feedback use Firebase. Photos you attach to feedback are sent by email after you confirm. Location-based address lookup uses Apple services.

Manage your privacy choices →

Who this policy covers

YU JIXUN develops Thailand Expat Assistant (外籍居民助手), App Store ID 6816998487. This policy describes version 1.0 of this app, including internal test builds. It does not apply the data practices of our other apps to this one. Contact: jhonbelleservice@outlook.com. The app is independent and is not operated by the Thai government or an immigration authority.

Personal records, photos and forms

Profiles and family details, passport information, visa and stay dates, arrival details, addresses, vehicle records, checklists, reminders and personal history are stored on your device. Passport and 90-day receipt text recognition runs on the device using Apple’s frameworks. These images and personal records are not automatically sent to our Firebase project. You choose the information to insert and the photos to keep. When you export or share a PDF, photo or backup, or print a form, the destination or printer you choose receives that content. Your device backup settings can also affect where app data is backed up.

Photos, clipboard and location

The system photo picker gives the app access to photos you select. Camera access, when offered and used, is subject to iOS permission. Address text is read when you paste it for recognition. Location is optional and used while the app is in use for nearby-office distances and obtaining an address. The app does not request background location. Distance calculations use the location on your device; reverse geocoding and maps may send location or search information to Apple. Opening navigation or a phone call uses the map or phone service you choose. GPS coordinates are not added to our usage statistics or automatically attached to feedback.

Optional usage statistics

Statistics are off by default. If you enable Share optional usage statistics in Settings, the app sends a daily summary to the developer’s Firebase project, operated by Google. It includes a randomly assigned Firebase identity, the UTC day, app version, iPhone/iPad device class, and predefined flags for app opening or main sections used. It does not include names, passport data, addresses, saved dates, document contents, currency amounts, selected offices or GPS location. The random identity is pseudonymous, not completely anonymous: it can associate submissions from the same installation. Firebase Authentication credentials are kept in the device Keychain. Turning statistics off stops future sends and clears the app’s unsent daily summary; it does not delete records already received.

Feedback, corrections and support email

Feedback is voluntary and sent when you choose Send. Text submissions can include your message, the selected office or material-guide context, fields you checked, revision information and compact diagnostics: app/version/build, device model, system version, language, appearance and time zone. They are stored privately in Firebase with a pseudonymous submission identity and server receipt time for review and support. Personal profiles, passport images and GPS are not automatically attached. Feedback with selected evidence photos is routed through email, not uploaded as photos to Firestore. The app prepares copies without EXIF/GPS metadata; visible private information in an image is not automatically redacted. You review and confirm sending through your mail or sharing service. Email exposes your sender/contact details and the content and attachments you choose to send to the developer and the relevant email providers. Local unsent feedback drafts can remain on the device until sent, discarded or removed.

Public reference data and service providers

The app downloads public office and holiday reference files from the developer’s GitHub Pages data feed and requests exchange-rate data from Frankfurter. Currency calculations and entered amounts stay on your device; rate requests include currency pairs or historical periods, not your personal profile. Network and hosting providers necessarily receive connection information such as IP address and request metadata. Apple provides system photo, maps, geocoding, printing and sharing features. Google provides Firebase Authentication and Firestore for the optional functions above. Microsoft/Outlook and your selected mail provider handle support email. Providers may process information outside your country under their own terms and policies.

Advertising, tracking and sharing

This app version does not include advertising SDKs, Google Analytics for Firebase or Crashlytics. We do not sell your personal information or use app data for cross-app advertising tracking. We use the optional information described above to provide support, review public reference corrections, understand feature use and improve the app. Access is limited to the developer and service providers supporting these purposes. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect security and legal rights. Reviewed factual corrections may become public reference data; your email address, submission identity, private message and evidence photos are not automatically published.

Backups and security

Manual backups are encrypted with the password you choose, and you select the save destination. We do not receive that password and cannot recover it. A Files or cloud destination may sync independently. Network submissions use HTTPS, and Firebase rules restrict access to feedback and usage records. No storage or transfer method guarantees absolute security. Keep your device and exported files protected, especially documents containing passport details.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them through the app or remove the app and its local data. Exported forms, photos, backups, device/cloud backups and Keychain credentials may remain separately and must be managed through the relevant system or provider. Submitted feedback, support emails and optional usage records are kept for support, reference-data review, security and product improvement while needed for those purposes. They are not configured to expire automatically after a fixed number of days; turning a setting off or uninstalling does not erase them. We delete or de-identify records when no longer needed, or following a valid deletion request, except where retention is legally necessary. Provider backups and logs may have separate retention periods.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can use the core app without optional statistics or feedback. Turn statistics off in Settings; manage location using the in-app switch and iOS permissions; manage notification and camera permissions in iOS Settings. Review or remove saved records and photos in the app, and separately delete exported files and backups where you saved them. To request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing, email jhonbelleservice@outlook.com with the app name and a brief description of your request. We may ask for minimal information, such as the approximate submission date or relevant feedback, to locate a record and verify the request; an email address alone may not identify pseudonymous usage records. Do not send a passport scan or password for this purpose. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to withdraw consent, object, restrict processing, portability, or complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect prior lawful processing.

Family records and children

The app is intended for adults managing their own or authorized family records. Only enter another person’s information if you are authorized, including appropriate parental or guardian authority for a child. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information for our optional online functions. Contact us if such information has been sent so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Website and policy updates

fundive.fun is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. These services receive normal technical request information such as IP address and browser details. These pages do not embed analytics, advertising or a feedback upload form. External destinations have their own privacy practices. We will publish changes here with an updated date; changes in an app version may require a revised policy. See Firebase privacy information, Google’s Privacy Policy, Apple’s Privacy Policy, Microsoft’s Privacy Statement, GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Google Fonts privacy information.

适用范围与联系我们

YU JIXUN 开发外籍居民助手（Thailand Expat Assistant），App Store ID 为 6816998487。本政策适用于本应用 1.0 版本及其内部测试版本，不将其他应用的数据处理方式套用到本应用。联系邮箱：jhonbelleservice@outlook.com。本应用独立运营，不代表泰国政府或移民机构。

本机资料、照片与表格

个人及家庭成员资料、护照信息、签证和停留日期、入境信息、地址、车辆、材料清单、提醒和个人记录保存在设备上。护照和 90 天报到回执的文字识别使用 Apple 框架在本机完成，不自动上传这些照片或个人档案到我们的 Firebase。由你选择填入哪些信息和保存哪些照片。导出、分享 PDF、照片或备份，或打印表格时，你选择的接收方、服务或打印机会收到相应内容。系统设备备份设置也可能影响应用资料的备份位置。

照片、剪贴板与定位

系统照片选择器仅向应用提供你选中的照片；使用可用的拍照功能时，摄像头权限由 iOS 管理。粘贴地址时读取你提供的地址文字。定位属于可选功能，只在使用期间用于计算附近移民局距离及获取地址，不申请后台定位。距离在本机计算；地址反向解析和地图功能可能将位置或搜索信息发送给 Apple。导航或拨号由你选择的地图、电话服务处理。GPS 坐标不会加入我们的使用统计，也不会自动附在反馈中。

可选使用统计

统计默认关闭。只有你在设置中开启分享可选使用统计，应用才会向开发者的 Google Firebase 项目发送每日摘要，包括随机 Firebase 身份标识、UTC 日期、应用版本、iPhone/iPad 设备类别，以及预设的应用开启或主要板块使用标记。不包括姓名、护照、地址、保存的日期、文件内容、输入金额、所选移民局或 GPS。随机标识可关联同一安装的记录，因此不属于完全匿名；Firebase 验证凭据保存在设备钥匙串。关闭统计会停止后续发送并清除本机未发送的每日摘要，不会自动删除服务器已收到的记录。

反馈、更正与支持邮件

反馈由你自愿点击发送。文字反馈可包含留言、所选移民局或材料清单的上下文、核实字段、资料修订信息，以及必要诊断：应用名称和版本、设备、系统、语言、外观和时区。这些信息连同随机提交身份和服务器接收时间，私密保存在 Firebase，用于核实与支持。个人档案、护照照片和 GPS 不自动附加。带证据照片的反馈通过邮件发送，照片不上传到 Firestore；应用生成的附件副本会去掉 EXIF/GPS 元数据，但不会自动遮挡照片画面中的私人信息。由你在邮件或分享服务中确认发送。开发者和邮件服务商会收到发送方联系方式、正文及所选附件。未发送的反馈草稿可在本机保留，直到发送、放弃或移除。

公开数据与第三方服务

应用从开发者的 GitHub Pages 数据源获取移民局和假期公开资料，并向 Frankfurter 请求汇率。换算及输入金额留在本机；汇率请求包含币种或历史期间，不附个人档案。网络服务商会接收 IP 地址及请求元数据等连接信息。Apple 提供照片、地图、地址解析、打印和分享系统服务；Google Firebase Authentication 和 Firestore 支持上述可选功能；Microsoft/Outlook 及你选择的邮件服务商处理支持邮件。服务商可能在你所在国家以外处理资料，并适用各自政策。

广告、追踪与对外提供

本版本未集成广告 SDK、Google Analytics for Firebase 或 Crashlytics。我们不出售个人资料，不将应用资料用于跨应用广告追踪。可选数据用于支持、公开信息更正审核、了解功能使用和改善应用；访问限于开发者及提供相应服务的供应商。法律要求或保护安全、合法权益时可能需要披露。审核后的事实性更正可能用于公开参考数据更新，但邮箱、随机身份、私人留言和证据照片不会自动公开。

备份与安全

手动导出的备份由你设置密码加密并选择保存位置。我们不接收密码，也不能找回。文件或云盘保存位置可能自行同步。网络提交使用 HTTPS，Firebase 规则限制反馈与统计访问；任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。请保护设备和导出的含护照等敏感信息的文件。

保存期限与删除

本机记录保存至你在应用中删除或移除应用及其本机数据。已导出的文件、照片、备份、系统或云端备份及钥匙串凭据可能独立保留，需要在相应位置管理。已提交的反馈、邮件与可选统计，在支持、公开资料核实、安全及产品改善所需期间保存；目前没有按固定天数自动到期删除的设置，关闭开关或卸载应用不会删除这些已收记录。资料不再需要时，或收到有效删除申请后，我们会删除或去标识化，法律要求保留的情况除外。服务商备份及日志可能另有保存周期。

隐私选择与资料申请

不分享统计、不发送反馈也可使用核心功能。可在设置中关闭统计，通过应用定位开关及 iOS 权限管理位置，并在 iOS 设置中管理通知和摄像头权限；在应用中删除不需要的记录及照片，另行管理导出的文件和备份。申请查阅、更正、删除或了解处理情况，请邮件联系 jhonbelleservice@outlook.com，写明应用名称及请求即可。我们可能需要大致提交日期或相关反馈等最少信息来定位记录和核实申请；邮箱本身未必能对应随机身份的统计。不要为此发送护照照片或密码。依适用法律，你可能还享有撤回同意、反对或限制处理、数据可携带及向监管机构投诉的权利。撤回不影响此前合法处理。

家庭成员与儿童信息

本应用面向管理本人或经授权家庭资料的成年人。填写他人资料前应获得授权；儿童资料需具备适当的父母或监护权限。我们不会主动向儿童收集可选在线功能所用的个人信息。若这些功能收到儿童信息，请联系我们核查并在适当情况下删除。

网站与政策更新

fundive.fun 使用 GitHub Pages 托管及 Google Fonts 字体，服务商会收到 IP 地址、浏览器等正常请求信息。本页面没有嵌入统计、广告或反馈上传表单。外部服务适用各自隐私政策。政策变更会在本页公布并更新日期，应用功能发生变化时可能需要修订。服务商政策链接见英文部分。
