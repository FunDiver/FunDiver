YOUR EVERYDAY PAPERWORK, TOGETHER

Prepare once.
Reuse your details.

Designed for people living in Thailand and the families they help. Personal records stay on your device.

01

Auto-fill & print forms

Use saved passport, arrival and address details in supported forms, including TM47. Preview, export a PDF or print. Signature fields stay blank for signing by hand.

02

Important date reminders

Keep 90-day reporting, visa validity, permitted stay and passport expiry separate. Manage family members and choose how far ahead to be reminded.

03

Nearby immigration offices

Browse offices by area, service and distance. Check phone numbers, addresses, opening hours and closure information. Call or start navigation.

04

Documents, ready to go

Prepare for common in-person procedures with document checklists. Save preparation progress and add a reminder when you need one.

05

Scan & save time

Read passport and 90-day receipt photos on your device. Review the results and choose the information to insert. Reuse saved addresses when filling forms.

06

Useful extras

Compare currency rates and their history. Organize vehicle insurance, tax, inspection and maintenance dates. Export an encrypted personal backup.

外籍居民助手

自动填表，打印 90 天报到表。
让在泰办事更方便。

选择家庭成员和常用地址，将已保存的护照及入境资料填入支持的表格，预览后导出 PDF 或打印，签名留待打印后手写。管理 90 天报到、签证有效期、本次允许停留期限和护照到期日；按距离查找移民局，查看联系电话、地址、办公时间和休息安排。

办事材料清单可以保存准备进度；护照和报到回执照片在本机识别。常用地址、汇率换算和车辆日期管理，让日常安排更集中。应用目前处于内部测试阶段。
