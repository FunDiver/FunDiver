ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

A little about the maker.

Independent projects, useful ideas, and a love of making everyday experiences a little better.

Cloud Music is one of those projects: an app for bringing a personal music collection together and keeping it close, online or offline.

Good tools should fit into your life, and leave more room for the things you enjoy.

Thanks for visiting and for supporting my work.
